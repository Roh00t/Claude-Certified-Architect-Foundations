What this drill is for

These questions target the eleven objectives your score report flagged — the ones you were not practising. Every question is newly authored against the objective; none reproduces exam content.

Two rules make this different from the main trainer:

  • You must rate your confidence before the answer reveals. Confident-and-wrong is the failure mode that cost you the exam, and you cannot detect it without committing first.
  • Options are length-neutralised. No difficulty toggle. Picking the longest or shortest option scores below random here, so the tell you were trained on is gone.
